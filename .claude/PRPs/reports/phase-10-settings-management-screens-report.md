# Implementation Report: Phase 10 — Settings & Management Screens

## Summary

## Deviations from Plan
- **`settings.themeVibrantDark` = "C. Koyu" / "V. Dark" from the start** — plan flagged clipping risk for "Canlı Koyu" in a 4-up 390px SegmentedToggle and pre-approved the shortening; applied directly rather than waiting for visual QA.
- **List screens use `paddingBottom: 40` instead of 120** — these are pushed stack screens without the bottom tab bar, so the tab-clearance padding from `GoalsScreen` is unnecessary.
